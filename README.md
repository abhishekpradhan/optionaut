# trading-helper-app

An explorable, visual guide to trading — pick a stock, see everything you could do with it (from buying shares to iron condors), and *understand* it by dragging strikes, scrubbing time, and watching the math respond.

**Status:** planning. The full product plan, research summary, and roadmap live in [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md).

Free, no signup, educational only — this app never places trades and never gives investment advice.

> Options involve a high degree of risk and are not suitable for all investors. Everything in this project is for educational purposes only and is not investment advice.
